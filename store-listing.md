## Description (4000)

Pit Boss runs your home poker night, from the first buy-in to the last payment.

It is not a poker game. No cards are dealt here and no money moves through the app. It is what you reach for when eight people are standing around a table at 1am and nobody agrees who owes what.

TRACK THE TABLE
Tap once to buy someone in. Tap again to reload them. The pot updates as you go, and the buy-in log keeps every entry with a timestamp.

REBUYS GET SIGNED
A reload at midnight is a loan, and it is forgotten by 1am. Pit Boss puts the name and the amount on screen in large type, takes a finger signature, and keeps it with the night. The person who reloaded is the person who agreed to it.

SETTLE IN THE FEWEST PAYMENTS
Enter everyone's final stack. Pit Boss checks the chips match the money, then works out the shortest set of transfers that clears every debt. Six players who could owe each other fifteen different ways usually settle in four.

PAY THE RIGHT WAY ROUND
Tell it which player is you and every row gets the right button: pay when you owe, request when you are owed. Venmo and Cash App open prefilled. Nothing is ever sent without you confirming it in their app.

SHARE IT
Send a link that opens the whole table in a browser. No app, no account, nothing for anyone to install. Or share the recap card: final standings, biggest swing, who reloaded the most.

KEEP THE HISTORY
Every night is saved. Running totals per player, results over time, and who still owes whom across nights.

ALSO IN THE BOX
• Chip denomination breakdown for any buy-in
• Bomb pot timer
• Player groups, so a regular table loads in one tap
• Cash someone out early without ending the night
• Correct a night afterwards, marked as edited so nobody is guessing
• Full backup and restore to a single file
• Two-panel layout on iPad in landscape

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account. No sign-up. No server, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, which is exactly why the backup is there.
